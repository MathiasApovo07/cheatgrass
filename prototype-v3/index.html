    How to use the tools

    Apply once per season, including the starting spring. No mitigation is available during the wildfire event between fall and winter. Leaving a season or switching modes cancels unconfirmed selections. The graph keeps its original annual spring samples. Hatched squares marked H remain treated through the same season next year; established plants and dead fuel remain.

        About this seasonal prototype

        This prototype lets you compare an invaded ecosystem with a pre-settlement ecosystem without cheatgrass. The annual graph records spring plant counts and wildfire events. Mode 3 adds Hoe, Seed Bag, Mower and Prescribed Burn. Pre-emergent Herbicide is available; fire animation and optional sound are available. Prescribed burns appear in the management log; the graph markers show natural wildfires. Explanations are draft text awaiting DRI review.

        Confirmed setup: sagebrush uses eight neighboring squares and only places seeds on empty squares. The initial spring is year 0; the first action enters summer. Reset starts a new trial.
