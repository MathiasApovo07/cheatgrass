HERBICIDE — 11 November 2026
Mode 3: select Pre-emergent Herbicide, drag with mouse or click two opposite corners, review the rectangle, then Apply tool. One action per season. H and hatching mark treated soil.
Seeds are not removed immediately: cheatgrass seeds disappear at winter germination, sagebrush seeds at spring germination. Existing plants remain. Dead cheatgrass whose seeds were suppressed stays as seedless fuel, including after expiry; burning it does not recreate seeds.
Duration interpretation for review: five seasonal transitions, through germination in the anniversary season; the marking then expires. Example: spring application blocks the following winter and spring germination. Overlap renews only selected cells. Reset clears treatment.
Verification: 50 Node tests pass, including minimal-DOM application handlers. No independent rendered-browser, touch or screen-reader verification in this environment. No publication performed.
