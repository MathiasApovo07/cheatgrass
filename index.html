Wildfire Mitigation Strategies

Choose one tool per season. Select targets, review the outlined squares, then Apply tool. You may also skip a season without using a tool.

Hoe: remove up to 5 plants of either species. Seed Bag: add up to 5 sagebrush seeds on empty squares. Mower: clear all plants and seeds from one row or column.

Apply once per season, including the starting spring. During Fire Season, use your tool before Roll d100. Leaving a season or switching modes cancels unconfirmed selections. The graph keeps its original annual spring samples.

        About this seasonal prototype

        This prototype lets you compare an invaded ecosystem with a pre-settlement ecosystem without cheatgrass. The annual graph records spring plant counts and wildfire events. Mode 3 adds Hoe, Seed Bag and Mower. Prescribed Burn and Chemicals await clarification; audio is a later step. Explanations are draft text awaiting DRI review.

        Provisional choices: sagebrush uses eight neighboring squares and only places seeds on empty squares. The initial spring is year 0; the first action enters summer. Reset starts a new trial.
